The Problem

Odoo reserves stock for the
wrong Manufacturing Orders

When multiple confirmed MOs compete for the same components, Odoo has no built-in mechanism to decide which one gets the stock first. A distant MO confirmed for November can block components needed for delivery tomorrow.

❌ Without MRP Smart Reservation

MO WH/MO/00015 scheduled for 01/11/2026 immediately locks 50 units of Comp A and Comp B. Urgent MOs due today or in 2 days show "Pas disponible" even though the stock is physically present — it's just blocked by a distant order.


📊 Stock forecast (Comp A) — before Smart Reservation: a distant MO locks all stock, urgent MOs cannot be served
The Solution

Priority-based automatic
stock rebalancing

MRP Smart Reservation introduces a 3-tier priority engine that automatically assigns stock to the most urgent orders first. Every MO gets a color-coded status badge so operators instantly know what is reserved and why.

📋 MO List view — Urgent MOs (green) are reserved first. Distant MOs (orange) are deprioritized — stock stays free for urgent needs.

✅ With MRP Smart Reservation

MOs WH/MO/00018 (Today), WH/MO/00017 (Dans 2 jours) and WH/MO/00016 (Dans 18 jours) are all marked Urgent and automatically reserved. The distant MO WH/MO/00015 (01/11/2026) stays Distant — it does not block any stock.

Priority 2 — Urgent

Urgent MOs are always
served first

Any MO scheduled within your configured horizon (default: 30 days) is classified as Urgent. It is automatically reserved in chronological order — soonest deadline first. Components show green "Disponible" immediately after confirmation.

✅ WH/MO/00017 — Dans 2 jours — Badge Urgent — Composants Disponibles
✅ WH/MO/00016 — Dans 18 jours — Badge Urgent — Composants Disponibles
Priority 3 — Distant + Force Override

Distant MOs never block stock —
unless you force it

MOs beyond your horizon are classified as Distant and are never reserved automatically. Their components remain available for urgent orders. But if you need to expedite one, a single click on ⚡ Force Reservation elevates it to top priority instantly.

⚡ WH/MO/00015 — 01/11/2026 — Badge Distant — Bouton Force Reservation visible — Stock non réservé automatiquement
Manual Control

Rebalance all reservations
in one click

The Rebalance Smart Reservations action is available directly from the MO list. Select all MOs and trigger a full rebalancing — the module unreserves everything and redistributes stock according to the priority rules. A notification confirms the result instantly.

🔄 Action menu → Rebalance Smart Reservations — triggered from the MO list after selecting all orders
Configuration

Set your horizon in seconds

Go to Manufacturing → Configuration → Settings and find the Smart Reservation section. Enter the number of days that defines your horizon. MOs beyond this window are Distant. MOs within it are Urgent. That's the only setting you need.

⚙️ Fabrication → Configuration → Paramètres → Smart Reservation — Horizon configurable (défaut : 30 jours)
Result

Stock correctly distributed
across all MOs

After Smart Reservation runs, the stock forecast shows the exact picture: urgent MOs are reserved in order, distant MOs wait. No more stock surprises on the factory floor.

📊 Prévision de stock finale — WH/MO/00018, 00017, 00016 réservés (urgent) · WH/MO/00019 et 00015 en attente (distant)
How it works

Three-tier priority engine

Every confirmed MO is classified automatically at each rebalancing cycle.

1
⚡ Forced

Manually Forced

Any MO manually elevated via the Force Reservation button. Reserved first, regardless of date. Stays forced until you cancel it.

2
✅ Urgent

Within Horizon

MOs scheduled within your configured horizon. Reserved automatically in chronological order — soonest deadline first.

3
⏸ Distant

Beyond Horizon

MOs scheduled far in the future. Never reserved automatically. Stock stays free for urgent needs. Can be forced manually at any time.
